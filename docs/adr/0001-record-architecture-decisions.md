# 0001 — Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-07-27

## Context

N-AALP makes several non-obvious, load-bearing choices. Future implementers and reviewers need to
know not just *what* was decided but *why*, and what alternatives were rejected.

## Decision

We keep Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) — one short document per load-bearing decision, with
context, decision, and consequences. ADRs are numbered and dated and are part of the published
documentation site.

## Consequences

A reviewer can trace any surprising design choice to its rationale. New ADRs are added as
decisions are made; existing ADRs are superseded, not rewritten.
